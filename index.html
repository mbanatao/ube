The brand direction

Distinctively ube. Designed for everyday.

Three principles guide the first release. We’ll use customer feedback and product validation—not unverified claims—to decide what earns a place on the final pack.

01

A recognizable Filipino flavor

Build the product story around ube, a flavor many Filipino consumers already know and enjoy.

02

A simple, memorable offer

Start with one chewy candy format, a clear pack size and pricing shoppers can quickly understand.

03

Trust before big claims

Use accurate ingredients, allergen, nutrition, shelf-life and regulatory information once verified for the finished product.

A disciplined launch

Prove the product. Then grow.

Our launch approach is designed to reduce avoidable risk before investing in wider distribution.

Stage 01

Develop & validate

Finalize the recipe and texture with a qualified manufacturer; test samples and gather structured consumer feedback.

Stage 02

Run a controlled pilot

Confirm cost, compliant labeling, shelf life, packaging and pilot retail performance before making scale claims.

Stage 03

Expand with evidence

Use repeat demand, retailer feedback, product quality and supply reliability to guide regional rollout decisions.

Ube 3-in-1 Coffee

The supplied sachet design is for an instant coffee product—not the chewy candy. We’ve included it as a companion product concept so the brand story stays accurate while both ideas can be developed.

Details shown on the supplied pack

  • Product: Ube-flavor 3-in-1 instant coffee
  • Net weight: 20 g per sachet
  • Sweetener claim: Stevia sugar / stevia sweetener
  • Preparation shown: Empty one sachet into a cup, add 150 ml hot water, stir well
  • Ingredients printed: Non-dairy creamer, coffee, stevia sweetener (steviol glycosides), ube flavor, maltodextrin, salt
  • Allergen statement printed: Contains milk
  • Storage printed: Store in a cool, dry place; avoid direct sunlight
  • Origin printed: Product of the Philippines

These details are transcribed from the provided artwork and are not independently verified. Confirm the final formula, ingredient order, allergen controls, nutrition panel, permitted claims, barcode, manufacturer details and Philippine FDA/label requirements before sale. The “healthier choice,” “lower calorie” and “no added refined sugar” claims should not be published as verified claims until substantiated and approved.

Brand architecture: Chewy Ube Candy remains the primary launch concept. Ube 3-in-1 Coffee is shown separately as a related beverage concept; the coffee sachet is not presented as candy packaging or as proof that either product is commercially approved.
